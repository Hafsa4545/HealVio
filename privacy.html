Healvio Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Healvio (“we”, “us”) is operated by Healvio (HMC), Bangalore, Karnataka, India. Healvio connects patients with registered Ayurveda, Homeopathy and Allopathy doctors for online consultations, and lets patients send prescriptions to medical stores. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who can see it, and your choices.

Not for emergencies. Healvio is not an emergency service. In an emergency, call 112 or go to the nearest hospital. Healvio is not a medical device and does not itself diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. Medical advice comes only from the doctors you consult.

1. Data we collect

  • Account details: name, phone number, email address, age, and your role (patient, doctor or medical store). You sign in with a one-time SMS code or your Google account.
  • Health information you share: the problem you describe, chat messages and images in a consultation, prescriptions, AI summaries you ask for, and ratings and reviews.
  • Doctor verification: photo, qualifications, registration number, hospital details, timings, certificates and signature.
  • Medical store details: store name, licence number, address and phone.
  • Medicine orders: the prescription you send and the store's availability reply and price.
  • Home delivery: if you choose home delivery, the delivery address, landmark, phone number and the map point you set are shared with the medical store that delivers your medicines. While a store delivers your order, the delivery phone's live location is shared with you so you can track it. It is visible only to you, that store and Healvio admins.
  • Payments: when you pay a doctor's fee or a medicine order, we keep the amount, the time and the PayPal payment number. We never see or store your card number, bank details or PayPal password.

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising.

2. How we use it

  • To create your account and verify your phone number or email.
  • To book, run and record consultations, and to issue prescriptions.
  • To send your prescription to a medical store, only when you choose to.
  • To verify doctors and stores before they appear in the app.
  • To keep the service safe, for example by reviewing doctors with consistently low ratings.
  • To send in-app and phone alerts about your bookings, payments, orders and reminders.

3. Who can see your data

  • Patients: the doctor you book sees your name, age, phone, problem description and consultation chat. A store sees your name, phone and medicines, only when you send it a prescription.
  • Doctors: patients see your public profile (photo, qualifications, hospital, timings, fee and reviews). Your certificates are seen only by Healvio administrators.
  • Healvio administrators can access records to verify providers, handle complaints and keep the service safe.

4. Services we use

  • Google Firebase (authentication and database), hosted on Google Cloud servers.
  • AI features: when you tap Summarize, Translate or AI doubt check, the consultation text and prescription are sent through our server to an AI model (Cloudflare Workers AI, or Anthropic Claude if we switch to it) to produce the answer. These providers do not use it to train AI. AI output is for understanding only and never replaces your doctor's advice.
  • AI call notes: during a video or voice call, while "AI notes" is on, each person's phone sends only their own voice, in short pieces, to the AI to turn it into text. The text is added to the consultation chat as "said on the call", which both people can see. The audio itself is not stored. Either person can tap "AI notes" on the call screen to stop it for their voice.
  • AI doctor finder and paper prescriptions: when you tap "Find the right doctor", the words you type are sent through our server to the AI to suggest a kind of doctor. When you photograph a paper prescription, the photo is sent to the AI to read the medicines, and the photo is saved with your medicine order so the store can check it. The AI does not keep the text or photo.
  • AI summary: when a consultation ends, our server sends the chat, the call notes and the prescription of that consultation to the AI (Cloudflare Workers AI) to write the summary. Only the patient and the doctor of that consultation can see it. The AI does not keep the text.
  • Reviews you write are public to Healvio users with your first name and last initial (for example "Ravi K."). The doctor can reply publicly.
  • Medicine reminders (only if you turn them on) are saved in your own account. Our server reads them only to send you a phone alert at each dose time. Only you can see them. "Read aloud" uses your phone's own voice and sends nothing to us.
  • Maps: delivery maps use OpenStreetMap, and the address is looked up from the map point with OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service. Directions open in Google Maps. These services receive only the map area or point being shown.
  • Payments are handled by PayPal. When you tap Pay, you approve the payment in PayPal's own window, and PayPal's privacy policy applies to the details you enter there. Healvio only receives the confirmation that the payment went through. Refunds are sent back to the same PayPal account.
  • Video and voice calls happen inside Healvio and go directly between the two phones, encrypted end to end. If a direct path is blocked, the encrypted call may pass through a relay server (ExpressTURN or Cloudflare), which cannot see or hear it. Calls are never recorded. Only the connection details needed to start a call are stored.
  • Phone notifications (only if you turn them on) are delivered by Google Firebase Cloud Messaging through our server. They contain the alert text. You can turn them off in your phone or browser settings at any time.

5. How long we keep data

We keep your account while it is active. Consultation records and prescriptions are kept for 3 years after the consultation, as Indian medical-record rules require, then deleted. You can delete your account at any time (see below).

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access rules ensure that each person can open only their own records, and that doctors and stores see only what is shared with them.

7. Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your personal data, and withdraw consent, by using the app or emailing hmc.info112@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

8. Delete your account

In the app: Profile → Delete my account → confirm. Your profile and login are deleted.
By email: write to hmc.info112@gmail.com from your registered email or phone number with the subject “Delete my Healvio account”. We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.
Some consultation and prescription records may be kept for the period required by law. They are then deleted.

9. Children

Patients under 18 must use Healvio with a parent or guardian, who consents on their behalf.

10. Grievance officer

Grievance Officer: HMC, Healvio, Bangalore, Karnataka, India. Email: hmc.info112@gmail.com. You can also tap Report a problem in the app. We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and resolve them within 15 days.

11. Changes

We will post any changes here and update the date above. Significant changes will be shown in the app. Please also read our Terms of Use.
