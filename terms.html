Healvio Terms of Use

Effective: 11 November 2026

Medical emergency? Do not use Healvio. Call 108 (ambulance) or go to the nearest hospital now. For mental-health crisis support call Tele-MANAS 14416.

1. What Healvio is

Healvio is a technology platform. It connects patients with registered medical practitioners (Allopathy, Ayurveda and Homeopathy) for online consultations by chat, voice and video, and lets patients send a prescription to a partner medical store for pick-up or home delivery. Healvio itself does not practise medicine and is not a hospital, clinic or pharmacy. Healvio is operated by Healvio (HMC), Bangalore, Karnataka, India.

2. The doctor is responsible for medical care

  • Every consultation, diagnosis, advice and prescription is given by the registered practitioner you consult. The practitioner (and their hospital or clinic) is responsible for their medical decisions and for following the Telemedicine Practice Guidelines, 2020 that apply to their system of medicine, their professional code of ethics and the Drugs and Cosmetics Act.
  • The practitioner decides whether an online consultation is suitable for your problem. They may ask you to visit in person.
  • Some medicines (Schedule X and narcotic or psychotropic medicines under the NDPS Act) cannot be prescribed in an online consultation.
  • Every Healvio prescription shows the practitioner's name, qualification, registration number and digital signature.

3. How Healvio checks doctors and stores

  • Before a doctor can be listed, the Healvio admin checks their registration number and documents. Each profile shows the doctor's name, qualification, registration number and hospital.
  • Medical stores are checked before they can receive orders. The store is responsible for dispensing medicines lawfully and only against a valid prescription.
  • Healvio can suspend any doctor or store that breaks these terms or the law.

4. Your consent

When you send a consultation request you agree to be consulted online, and you confirm that the details you give are true and are about you (or a person you are allowed to act for). You can stop a consultation at any time.

5. AI features help, they do not treat

  • Healvio's AI only assists: it can suggest which kind of doctor to see, take notes of what each person said on a call, translate, read a paper prescription so you can check it, and summarize what the doctor said.
  • The AI never diagnoses, prescribes or replaces your doctor. If the AI and your doctor differ, follow your doctor. Check AI text with your doctor if anything is unclear.
  • You or the doctor can switch off AI call notes during a call. Audio is not stored.

6. Fees, payments and refunds

  • The doctor sets the consultation fee. You pay it in the app (PayPal) before the consultation starts. The doctor may also mark a consultation as free or paid outside the app.
  • Full refund if you cancel before the consultation starts, or if the doctor cancels. A refund goes back to the same PayPal account.
  • Medicine prices and delivery fees are set by the medical store. If the store cancels a paid order, you get a full refund.
  • Healvio never sees your card or PayPal password.

7. Your responsibilities

  • Give true information about your health, medicines and allergies.
  • Do not misuse the app: no abuse, fake bookings, fake reviews, or sharing someone else's account.
  • Reviews must be honest and about your own consultation. Healvio may remove reviews that are abusive or false.

8. Complaints (grievance redressal)

Tap "Report a problem" on any consultation or medicine order in the app. Only the Healvio admin sees your report. We acknowledge it within 24 hours and resolve it within 15 days. You can see the reply in Profile → My reports. You can also email our Grievance Officer (HMC) at hmc.info112@gmail.com. Serious misconduct by a practitioner can also be reported to their medical council.

9. Privacy

How we collect, use and protect your data is explained in our Privacy Policy. Your health records are visible only to you, the doctor or store you share them with, and the Healvio admin.

10. Limits of liability

Healvio works hard to keep the service available and secure, but it is provided "as is". Healvio is not responsible for the medical advice or prescriptions of practitioners, or for medicines supplied by stores, except where the law says otherwise.

11. Changes and law

We may update these terms and will show the new date at the top. These terms are governed by the laws of India.
